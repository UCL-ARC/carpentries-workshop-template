UCL's Advanced Research Computing Centre provides training to staff at UCL to help get their work done in less time and with less pain by teaching them basic research computing skills. This hands-on workshop is based on The Carpentries material, and may cover basic concepts and tools, including program design, version control, data management, and task automation. Participants will be encouraged to help one another and to apply what they have learned to their own research problems.

For more information on what we teach and why, please see The Carpentries paper "Best Practices for Scientific Computing".
